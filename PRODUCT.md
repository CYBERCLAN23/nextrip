# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

African and international students exploring study-abroad options, their parents seeking a credible guide, and scholarship candidates comparing institutions and specialties.

## Product Purpose

Help students understand opportunities, compare universities and specialties, prepare admissions and scholarship applications, and contact NexTrip for guided study in China.

## Positioning

NexTrip pairs a China-first university, admissions, and scholarship discovery experience with structured human guidance. Canada and Germany are designed as clearly marked future destinations, not unavailable dead ends.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide React are the active stack.
- Current live destination: China.
- Future destinations: Canada and Germany, each with an explicit “coming soon” state.
- Required capabilities: contact and application forms, university search, filters by specialty/country/level, administrable blog, scholarships, testimonials, and received forms.
- The site must be responsive, SEO-conscious, fast, secure, and able to expand without a redesign.
- The existing stack is Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Lenis, Anime.js, and React Three Fiber.

## Brand Commitments

- NexTrip Global Limited is modern, professional, international, clear, structured, reassuring, and premium.
- The user requests an exceptional 2025–2026 visual standard with bespoke 3D and considered motion, not a generic education-template appearance.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing study-in-China, admissions, scholarships, universities, blog, contact, and supporting route shells.
- A locally available 3D globe asset and React Three Fiber globe scene.

## Product Principles

- Give students a clear next step from the first screen.
- Make China, admissions, scholarships, and university matching immediately understandable.
- Keep every future-market state honest and actionable.
- Motion must reveal structure and confidence, not obscure information.
